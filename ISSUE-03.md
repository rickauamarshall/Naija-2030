## Club Form Is the Starting Point, Not the Verdict

Club achievement should earn a player attention, not settle the international argument. The last two cycles offer a recurring question: when a player arrives with an outstanding club résumé, does that form translate into responsibility, influence, and decision-making for Nigeria in high-stakes matches?

Ademola Lookman's club honors and individual recognition make the question especially clear, but the standard should apply across the squad. We should track the international evidence without reducing a player to one match: role, availability, chance creation, defensive work, combinations, and response to pressure. The useful comparison is not club success versus international failure. It is whether the national-team role gives the player the conditions to reproduce what made the club season valuable, and whether the player meets that moment.
