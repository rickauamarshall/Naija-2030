Two Goals, Two Surgeries, and a Very Different November

Preview: Arokodare is flying. Osimhen and Chukwueze have changed the selection conversation. Tanzania is next.

The last European week before Tanzania had everything: Tolu Arokodare scoring twice, Moses Usor making things happen, Ademola Lookman walking into another big-game audition—and then the injury news landed like a cold bucket of water.

Victor Osimhen is scheduled for left-knee surgery. Samuel Chukwueze is already in left-hip rehabilitation. Suddenly, the forward line is less of a luxury problem and more of a live puzzle. 🧩

The quick version

  • 🚨 Osimhen: left-knee surgery is scheduled Saturday; no recovery timeline yet. Club statement.
  • 🩹 Chukwueze: left-hip rehabilitation has begun. Milan update.
  • 🔥 Arokodare: two goals in Ajax’s 6–1 win at Midtjylland. Ajax report.
  • ⚡ Usor + Adeniran: assist and goal in LASK’s 4–0 win over Slovan. LASK report.
  • 🎯 Gift Orban: nine league goals and four assists, with Galatasaray next. Galatasaray preview.
  • 📈 Tyrique George: created Everton’s decisive second goal against Coventry. Everton report.

🚨 The headline nobody ordered

Osimhen started as Galatasaray beat Stuttgart 2–0. By Friday, the club had announced a left-knee injury, MRI evidence of a loose body, and surgery for Saturday. He is out of Galatasaray’s Amed S.F. match; Nigeria have not yet announced his international status. The club’s medical update.

Chukwueze’s left-hip tendon operation was successful and rehabilitation begins in Italy. Two established attackers, two different surgeries, one very awkward week for any selection meeting. AC Milan.

🔥 The form file: who made noise?

Tolu Arokodare: two goals, no need for a drum roll

Ajax won 6–1 at Midtjylland. Arokodare struck in the ninth and 52nd minutes, then came off after 70. Two goals before the window is not a referendum; it is, however, a pretty loud knock on the door. Ajax’s report.

  • What pops: movement in the box, decisive finishing, early impact.
  • What we want next: one more strong club performance, then the actual Nigeria squad—not a rumor carousel.

November read: with Osimhen’s availability now in doubt, Arokodare moves ahead of Akor Adams in the immediate striker conversation. That is a window-specific selection read, not a permanent Big Board reshuffle.

Gift Orban: the loudest club form we were about to miss

Orban scored in Amedspor’s 5–1 win over Eyüpspor, then walked into the week with nine league goals and four assists. Saturday’s visit to Galatasaray is a deliciously inconvenient audition: a chance to make the point against the league leaders, with Nigeria’s No. 9 situation suddenly much less comfortable. Eyüpspor result · Galatasaray preview.

Tyrique George: Everton’s minutes are becoming a message

George started Everton’s 3–1 win over Coventry and supplied the cross for Thierno Barry’s second goal. It validates his current No. 28 Big Board placement rather than demanding a jump: he is receiving real Premier League responsibility, but England’s U21 pathway still makes this a recruitment watch, not a victory lap. Everton report.

Moses Usor + Samuel Adeniran: LASK left the light on

LASK put four past Slovan Bratislava. Usor’s corner created the opener; Adeniran scored the fourth. A useful reminder that the player pool is not only watching the biggest five badges in Europe. LASK’s report.

  • Usor: creation and repeated threat.
  • Adeniran: goal, minutes, momentum.
  • The honest next question: can either carry that energy into the league, then into a Nigeria conversation?

Ademola Lookman: Barcelona awaits

Lookman entered in the 59th minute of Atlético’s 0–2 defeat to Bayern. A loss is a loss; a high-profile home match against Barcelona is the next chance to see whether his role expands before the break. Atlético match center.

🧮 The Tanzania math, minus the headache

Guinea-Bissau lead Group L on six points. Nigeria have three. Tanzania are already through as co-hosts, which means Nigeria, Guinea-Bissau, and Madagascar are fighting for one non-host spot. No cushion. No “we’ll sort it out later” energy. CAF’s Group L update.
