## Nwaneri is a watch. Francis Onyeka is now on the board. Tanzania is next.

Nigeria's next two AFCON 2027 qualifiers are Tanzania twice: Uyo on Nov. 13, then Dar es Salaam on Nov. 17. Nigeria sit second in Group L, three points behind Guinea-Bissau, and only one team qualifies from the group. [NFF fixture update](https://thenff.com/pamoja-2027-race-eagles-falter-lose-0-3-to-wild-dogs-in-bissau/)

The Nwaneri news is worth watching, but it is still just that. Reports say Éric Chelle has made contact with Ethan Nwaneri's camp. We have not found a player or NFF statement confirming that Nwaneri has chosen Nigeria, so there is no “switch complete” story here. He stays `WATCH`.

There is a new name at the end of the Big Board: Francis Onyeka. Bundesliga says the Elversberg midfielder was born in Gummersbach to Nigerian parents. He has represented Germany at youth level, including competitive youth football. That makes him a real player to track, not a player to announce. Any Nigeria route would need a formal eligibility review.
