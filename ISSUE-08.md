## Nwaneri watch, a weekend pulse, and Tanzania next

The next Super Eagles window is set. Nigeria host Tanzania in Uyo on November 13, then head to Dar es Salaam four days later. These are AFCON 2027 qualifiers, and they are not routine: Tanzania are already through as co-hosts, so Nigeria are competing with Guinea-Bissau and Madagascar for the one other qualifying place in Group L. [NFF fixture update](https://thenff.com/pamoja-2027-race-eagles-falter-lose-0-3-to-wild-dogs-in-bissau/) · [CAF qualifying format](https://www.cafonline.com/afcon2025/news/the-road-to-east-africa-mapped-out-the-qualifier-draw-for-the-totalenergies-caf-africa-cup-of-nations-pamoja-2027-concluded/)

The other big Super Eagles story this week is not a completed switch. It is the possibility of one.

Multiple outlets report that Éric Chelle has spoken with Ethan Nwaneri's representatives about Nigeria. That is meaningful recruitment work and exactly the kind of pathway the Tracker exists to watch. It is **not** a commitment: no NFF or player statement confirming a Nigeria decision has been located. Nwaneri remains in England's U21 pathway, after joining England's senior World Cup preparation camp earlier this year.

- **Reported development:** Nwaneri is considering Nigeria after reported contact with Chelle's camp. [Afri-Global report](https://www.afriglobalnews.com/ethan-nwaneri-reportedly-considers-nigeria-switch-as-super-eagles-renew-interest/)
- **Verified current pathway:** England called Nwaneri into its senior preparation camp in May; he is also listed by Bundesliga as an England U21 player. [England](https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2026/May/22/young-england-talents-to-join-three-lions-in-florida) · [Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/arsenal-ethan-nwaneri-borussia-dortmund-loan-deal-injury-ole-book-38938)
- **Tracker classification:** WATCH. No eligibility label, pool placement, or rank changes are proposed from reported talks alone.

- **Window:** Nov. 9–17, 2026. [CAF calendar](https://www.cafonline.com/afcon2025/news/totalenergies-caf-afcon-pamoja-2027-md-1-2-qualifiers-fixtures-kick-off-times-and-venues/)

## NFF hierarchy: what is known

FIFA's association page currently lists **no NFF president** and names Emmanuel Ikpeme as acting general secretary, David Atabo as treasurer, Ademola Olajire for media and communications, Augustine Eguavoen as technical director, and Éric Chelle as men's national coach. [FIFA association record](https://inside.fifa.com/associations/NGA/organisation)

Separately, Premium Times reports FIFA approved a Normalisation Committee, with FIFA and CAF expected to jointly appoint its members. Until a formal appointment list is published, do not report a completed committee or assume its personnel. [Premium Times](https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/headlines/919379-fifa-approves-normalisation-committee-to-run-nigerian-football.html)

The editorial question is not whether one reported approach lands a player. It is whether a transparent, stable football administration can turn recruitment leads into a repeatable process—with ownership, follow-through, and clear communication.

## Performance-stat posting rhythm

The repository's post formatter remains **mock-mode only** and this environment does not currently have `npm` available, so no automated live stat feed can be validated or activated today. Start manually and only automate after a licensed data source and X write credentials are configured.

### Today’s operating cadence

1. **Final whistle + official/competition confirmation:** publish one factual player line only when result, minutes, and event credits are confirmed.
2. **One tracker read:** add one sentence on role, availability, or what must happen next. Never convert a single line into a rank change.
3. **Evening pulse:** one six-to-ten-player roundup, using only already-confirmed lines.
4. **Next-morning correction window:** correct an assist, minute total, or selection error visibly if the primary record differs.

### Source hierarchy for the feed

1. Club, competition, federation, or player statement.
2. Licensed competition event/appearance record.
3. Reputable outlet, labelled “reported,” when the primary record is not yet available.
4. Never use an X fan clip or fan account as the sole source for a statistic.
