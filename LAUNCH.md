# NAIJA 2030 — Issue №1
**September 18, 2026**

## The Rebuild Starts Now

This is either the most important week in Nigerian football
administration in a decade, or another false start — and nobody knows
which yet.

Three weeks ago, NFF president Ibrahim Musa Gusau and the entire
Executive Committee resigned. President Tinubu immediately ordered the
National Sports Commission to run a "comprehensive reform" — governance,
electoral structure, development pathways, the works. The NSC wants a
temporary Normalisation Committee to run things until fresh elections.
State FA chairmen are pushing back, insisting the existing structures
should fix themselves instead. And right now, a FIFA delegation is in
Abuja deciding whether to bless the whole plan or send it back to the
drawing board — their answer is expected "in the coming days."

This is exactly the fight NAIJA 2030 exists to track. We'll keep you
posted the moment FIFA rules.

## AFCON Qualifying — Two Games That Matter

Nigeria plays **Madagascar (Sept 25)** and **Guinea-Bissau (Sept 29)** in
back-to-back AFCON 2027 qualifiers. Chelle has named a full-strength
squad — Osimhen, Lookman, Iwobi, Ndidi, Onyedika, Chukwueze all included,
plus goalkeeper Maduka Okoye between the sticks.

Then things get interesting: **October 6 vs. Russia** is a completely
different story.

## The Russia Friendly: Chelle Empties the Bench

No Osimhen. No Lookman. No Iwobi, Bassey, or Aina. Chelle has named a
deliberately experimental 24-man squad for the Russia friendly, handing
debuts to players most fans have never heard of: Switzerland-based
defender **Victory Akpe**, Finland-based goalkeeper **Samuel James**, and
Czechia-based fullback **Abdullahi Bewene**. Captain duties fall to
Ndidi, with Moses Simon and Nwabali as the senior heads in the room.

This is precisely the "big board" thesis in action — Chelle is using a
low-stakes friendly to stress-test the next tier of the pool. We'll be
watching every one of them.

## Weekend Fixtures — Pool Players to Watch

**Sat, Sept 19**
- **Trabzonspor vs. Galatasaray** — Osimhen, on a 6-goals-in-4-starts
  tear this season, on the road.

**Sun, Sept 20**
- **Juventus vs. Atalanta** — the storyline of the weekend. This isn't
  Ahanor vs. Ekhator anymore (Ahanor moved to Crystal Palace on loan from
  Chelsea), but Ekhator himself remains one of our biggest urgency cases:
  Juventus's new signing, still Nigeria-eligible, currently in Italy's
  U21 setup.
- **Fulham vs. Manchester United** — Iwobi and Bassey, both trying to
  arrest Fulham's rough patch, against a marquee name.
- **Besiktas vs. Amed SFK** — Ndidi captaining in the league.
- **AC Milan vs. Lecce** — Chukwueze, San Siro.
- **Atlético Madrid vs. Real Madrid** — the actual Madrid Derby.
  **Lookman starts in one of the biggest club fixtures in world football
  this weekend.**

## Weekend Form Check

- **Osimhen** continues to terrorize the Süper Lig — 6 goals in 4 starts
  this season for Galatasaray, most recently a goal in a 3-2 win at
  Başakşehir.
- **Lookman** has had a mixed start at Atlético Madrid — 1 goal in 5
  league starts, and took some criticism in the press after a recent
  defeat to Real Sociedad.
- **Iwobi** is quietly struggling at Fulham this season, per multiple
  Nigerian outlets flagging his dip in form.

## The Dual-National Battle — This Week Alone

- 🟢 **David Ozoh** (Derby County, 21) — chose Nigeria over England
  **two days ago**. Awaiting passport and FIFA clearance, could be in the
  frame for the Russia friendly if it's processed in time.
- 🟢 **Lesley Ugochukwu** (Galatasaray, ex-Chelsea) — actively pursuing a
  Nigerian passport since July, spotted playing football with Iheanacho
  in Owerri over the summer. This is the real deal, not just speculation.
- 🟢 **Arthur Okonkwo** (Wrexham GK) — FIFA officially cleared his switch
  and he's now a regular in the squad. One of our biggest wins this year.
- 🔴 **Honest Ahanor** (now Crystal Palace, on loan from Chelsea) — has
  two senior caps for Italy already (both friendlies, vs. Luxembourg and
  Greece), but at 18 with only 2 caps he remains eligible for FIFA's
  one-time-switch rule. This is a real, formal mechanism Nigeria could
  still pursue — not just hope.
- 🔴 **Jeff Ekhator** (Juventus) — same profile as Ahanor: Nigeria-
  eligible, currently in Italy's U21 setup.
- 🔴 **Luca Koleosho, Michael Kayode** — Italy is reportedly moving to
  lock in both as they rebuild post-World-Cup-failure.
- 🔴 **Noah Atubolu** (Freiburg GK) — just entered Klopp's senior Germany
  setup. Still technically uncapped, but the door is closing fast.
- 🟡 **Samuel Aghehowa** (Porto) — made one competitive appearance for
  Spain, but FIFA's one-time-switch rule (under-21, ≤3 caps) means he's
  *still eligible* for Nigeria. Eguavoen reportedly has family ties on
  his mother's side.
