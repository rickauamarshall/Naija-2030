# European Match-Week Content Cadence

Standing workflow for Super Eagles Tracker during UEFA European match weeks
(UCL Tue/Wed, UEL/UECL Thu). Established 15 Oct 2026.

## Midweek: European nights

- **Lineup sweeps** — check CONFIRMED starting XIs ~1 hour before kickoff per
  window (e.g. Thursday 11:45am ET for the 12:45pm ET games, 2pm ET for the
  3pm ET games). Report to chat; flag surprises vs predicted XIs. Never print
  a predicted XI as confirmed. Settle conflicting availability reports from
  the teamsheet, not from previews.
- **Stat pack (next morning)** — verified numbers for Nigerian performers.
  Two independent sources per figure; single-source figures labelled
  indicative. Table positions only after ALL matchday fixtures are final —
  provisional post-Tuesday numbers go stale overnight (Oct 14: Gala 34th→35th,
  Atlético 13th→15th after Wednesday's games).
- **Review posts** — objective stats reporting. No superlatives, no
  editorializing ("numbers don't care about narratives"-style lines are cut);
  Babski adds narrative himself. Drafts only until he approves posting.

## Friday AM: weekend preview

- Profile 5–8 pool players with notable weekend fixtures (form, rivalry,
  response game, debut watch, availability watch).
- Draft Thursday; goes out Friday morning.

## Monday AM: weekend review

- Follow up on exactly the profiled players: minutes, stats, how they did.
- Draft Monday morning.

## Applies on European match weeks

This cadence repeats on UEFA European match weeks (UCL/UEL/UECL league-phase
matchdays). On off weeks, fall back to the regular newsletter rhythm.

## Standing rules (all cadences)

- Drafts only: nothing posted, published, scheduled, or sent without
  Babski's explicit approval.
- Stats need two sources; unverified superlatives get cut before review.
- Carousel photos: real match photos from clubs'/players' official accounts;
  verify the face in the actual image, never trust the caption; if a photo
  shows the wrong player, find the right one — never relabel.
- Keep Super Eagles Tracker separate from Fadius; never conflate with
  EaglesTracker.
- Babski's public anonymity is absolute in all public copy.
