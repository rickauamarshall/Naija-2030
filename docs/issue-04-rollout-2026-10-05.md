### 1

Nigeria's Russia friendly was supposed to be a clean experiment.

Instead, it became a process test.

The NFF announced 24 players on Sept. 17. By Oct. 4, club restrictions, injuries, and a permit issue had reduced the group to 18.

That matters.

### 2

The absences:

Ndidi - injured
Victory Akpe - injured
Onyedika - club restriction
Osayi-Samuel - club restriction
Ridwan Abdulrasaq - club restriction
Moses Usor - club restriction
Suleiman Mubarak - permit issue

Per NFF.

### 3

Some club-release friction is normal.

Players carry knocks. Clubs worry about minutes, travel, and medical risk. A friendly after a competitive window is exactly where resistance can show up.

But this many unresolved issues two days before kickoff is not a small detail.

### 4

FIFA's rules make the broad principle clear: clubs are obliged to release players during listed international windows.

But the process also depends on written notices, confirmation timelines, travel details, and escalation.

That is why this is a process story.

### 5

From the outside, we cannot prove which piece failed for each player.

Maybe some clubs resisted.
Maybe some paperwork moved late.
Maybe some travel/permit details were not settled early enough.

The outcome still looks messy.

### 6

The Russia squad also exposed a tracker question.

Ten players in the current 18 were not in our live pool or Big Board before this update:

Atata, Igboke, Fernandez, Nwoke, Stephen Michael, Igor Wilfred, Adeoye, Edokpolor, Abubakar Umar, Abdullahi Umar.

### 7

We checked the ten.

Our view: do not add every player just because Chelle called him.

But five are serious enough to consider now:

Emmanuel Fernandez
Chibuzo Nwoko
Wilfred Igor George
Umar Abubakar
Abdullahi Umar

### 8

The other five should probably stay squad-only or post-match watchlist for now:

Michael Atata
Kenneth Igboke
Peter Edokpolor
Stephen Michael
Aderemi Adeoye

Not dismissals. Just evidence discipline.

### 9

That is the point of the tracker.

An experimental call-up is evidence, not a verdict.

The next question is whether the player profile, club level, minutes, and match performance justify a real pool/Big Board move.

### 10

Four players we would have liked to see in this kind of friendly, pending club clearance:

GK: Maduka Okoye
DEF: Chibuike Nwaiwu
MID: David Ozoh
FWD: Zadok Yohanna

That is the kind of test a low-stakes friendly should create.

### 11

This is what the USMNT just got from a young/fresh friendly squad: young players scoring, records falling, and Pochettino suddenly having harder selection decisions.

That is the good version of an experimental window.

### 12

The question for Tuesday is not just "who plays well?"

It is:

What did Chelle actually learn?
Which new names are real pool candidates?
And why did so much of the original plan fail so close to kickoff?
