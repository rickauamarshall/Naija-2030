    Why this exists

    This project exists to foster honest, constructive discourse about the current state of Nigerian football. The Super Eagles have missed the last two World Cups. The Super Falcons — ten-time African champions — missed automatic qualification for the 2027 World Cup for the first time since the tournament began in 1991. Nigeria has too much talent, too much history, and too much footballing pride for either to be true. This is a public, transparent pool of every player and technical mind Nigeria could call on — including the dual nationals we haven't convinced yet — so our fans have a real reason to wear the green and white with pride, and our federation has no excuse left to hide behind.

    How the ranking works

    Weekly composite score = 40% club output (minutes, G/A or position-weighted defensive actions, adjusted for league strength) + 30% market value trend (Transfermarkt value and its 90-day delta) + 20% eligibility urgency (uncapped players close to a cap-tying appearance elsewhere rank higher, all else equal) + 10% age curve (projected form in 2030, not now — this is what pushes 30-somethings down the board over time). Every score is versioned so risers/fallers are visible week over week. Methodology is published in full, on purpose — a credible advocacy tool has to show its work.

    A note on subjectivity

    The formula above is a starting point, not a vending machine — final rank and pool placement are one person's editorial call, informed by the score but not bound by it. When I move a player against what the composite score alone would say, that's a judgment call I'm making publicly, not a bug. This is opinionated, advocacy-driven sports commentary — closer to a power rankings column than a neutral database — and it's presented that way on purpose rather than dressed up as objective. The eligibility badges (OPEN/WATCH/TIED) are the one exception: those are fact claims about real people, not opinion, and stay held to the separate human-verification bar described above regardless of what any ranking judgment call decides.
