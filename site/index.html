    Nigerian Descent, Other Flags

    Why this exists

    This project exists to foster honest, constructive discourse about the current state of Nigerian football. The Super Eagles have missed the last two World Cups. The Super Falcons — ten-time African champions — missed automatic qualification for the 2027 World Cup for the first time since the tournament began in 1991. Nigeria has too much talent, too much history, and too much footballing pride for either to be true. This is a public, transparent pool of every player and technical mind Nigeria could call on — including the dual nationals we haven't convinced yet — so our fans have a real reason to wear the green and white with pride, and our federation has no excuse left to hide behind.
